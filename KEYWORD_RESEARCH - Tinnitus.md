## 3. Dominant Search Intent and Audience Expectations

All interpretations in this section are **[INFERRED]** from the service, location, conversion goal, and site content. No search data supports them.

### Likely visitor goals
1. **Make the noise stop or become manageable** — directly supported by the site's relief and masking language (S3).
2. **Find out what is causing it** — the site frames cause-finding as the first step (S3).
3. **Find someone local and trustworthy** in or near Victoria.
4. **Sleep better** — the site links tinnitus with sleep trouble (S3); motivation is inferred.
5. **Check whether it is linked to hearing loss** — hearing loss is a listed cause (S3).

### Main problems and needs
Ringing, buzzing, whooshing, hissing, or clicking; constant or intermittent; difficulty sleeping; emotional strain ranging from anxiety to anger (S3). Possible accompanying hearing difficulty (S11, S20).

| Who they will see | Yes — Nick Yendrey, HIS (S8); tinnitus experience **[MISSING]** |
| What a visit involves | Partially — general evaluation details (S10); tinnitus-specific steps **[MISSING]** |
| Cost / insurance | Partially — financing (S18, S19); insurance and prices **[MISSING]** |
| How to call or book | Yes — phone and form (S1) |

### Commercial vs. informational intent
- **Commercial / local:** service term + "Victoria" or "near me." Closest fit to a call goal.
- **Solution-exploring:** "hearing aids for tinnitus," "masking." Mixed — interested but evaluating.
- **Informational:** "why are my ears ringing," "how to stop ringing in ears." Mostly educational; weak call fit unless combined with a local or persistence signal.

### Conversion motivations — [INFERRED]
Frustration with a persistent sound; sleep disruption; worry about what it means; preference for a nearby, established, family-owned provider; a low-commitment first step (evaluation or consultation).

### Important unknowns
Real query mix; share of medical-seeking searchers ("doctor," "ENT"); whether Spanish-language queries matter to this campaign; whether any searchers are already Brown patients.
